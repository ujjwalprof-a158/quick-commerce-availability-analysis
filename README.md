# quick-commerce-availability-analysis
“Out-of-stock rates and stock depth across 3,257 quick-commerce products, after removing duplicate categories.”


Project bullets

Identified that out-of-stock rates in quick-commerce vary by category, with Biscuits (28.1%), Dairy (21.7%), and Meats (19.1%) highest, by cleaning a 3,732-product file and calculating category-level rates with COUNTIFS and PivotTables.
Reconciled 3,257 unique products to a total of 403 out-of-stock items, by removing 342 duplicated Paan Corner rows and 129 duplicated Beverages rows, and checking each category count against a PivotTable total.
Tested whether low stock or poor distribution drove stock-outs, by replacing a circular stock-depth measure with an in-stock-only average across 12 categories.


file used - [Zepto_availbility and pricing analysis.xlsx](https://github.com/user-attachments/files/34953817/Zepto_availbility.and.pricing.analysis.xlsx)
